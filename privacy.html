Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 4, 2026

1. App Overview

Daily Kannada is a mobile application that helps non-Kannada speakers learn essential Kannada phrases used in daily life in Karnataka.

2. Information We Collect

The app may collect the following information:

  • Email address (only if you create an account)
  • Favorite phrases saved by you
  • Phrase reports submitted by you
  • Phrase suggestions submitted by you

3. How We Use Information

Collected information is used only to:

  • Provide app functionality
  • Save your preferences and favorites
  • Review and improve phrase accuracy

4. Data Storage and Security

User data is stored securely and used only for application functionality. We apply reasonable technical measures to protect data from unauthorized access.

5. User Rights

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Please visit the Delete Data Policy page for available options.
