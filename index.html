Daily Kannada

Learn essential Kannada phrases quickly and communicate confidently in Karnataka.

Daily Kannada is designed for newcomers, travelers, and professionals who want practical phrase support for daily life in Karnataka. The app focuses on real conversations with Kannada text, English translation, transliteration, and audio pronunciation.

Feature Highlights

  • Learn useful Kannada phrases
  • Practice with flashcards
  • Search and listen to pronunciation
  • Save your favorite phrases
  • Report incorrect phrases
  • Suggest new phrases
  • Offline phrase access
  • Dark and light mode support

About the App

The app is built for practical, everyday communication. It helps users navigate common situations such as taxis, restaurants, shops, workplaces, and daily interactions with locals.

Users may optionally create an account to save favorites and submit phrase reports or new phrase suggestions.
