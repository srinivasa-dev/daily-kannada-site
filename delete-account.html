Delete Data Policy

Last updated: March 4, 2026

How to Request Deletion

You can delete your account in either of the following ways:

  1. Inside the app: Go to Settings and choose the Delete Account option.
  2. By email: Send a deletion request to dk-support@srinivasa.dev from your registered email address.

What Happens After Deletion

The app uses soft deletion. When your account is deleted:

  • Your account is marked as deleted in the database.
  • Your account becomes inactive.
  • You can no longer log in using that account.
  • Your data is hidden from the application.

Temporary Retention

Some data may be temporarily retained for technical, legal, or security reasons before permanent removal.

Re-Registration with the Same Email

If a deleted user signs up again with the same email address, the account may be restored.

Need Help?

For support, contact dk-support@srinivasa.dev.
